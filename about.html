Our Story

We got tired of ordering from five different apps.

CARTIFY started in 2022 with a simple complaint: why does buying a t-shirt, a face serum, and a phone charger mean three different apps, three different logins, and three different deliveries? We built one store instead — clothing, footwear, beauty, home essentials and electronics, picked by one team, shipped from one cart.

Curated, not endless

We'd rather list 25 products we trust across every category than 25,000 we haven't checked.

One cart, one checkout

Clothing, electronics, beauty — all in the same order, the same delivery window, the same support line.

Real people behind returns

No chatbot maze. If something's wrong with your order, a real person sorts it out.

"We buy groceries, clothes and electronics across different apps — we shouldn't have to for everything else too."
— Founder's note, 2022
